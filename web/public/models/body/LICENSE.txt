young-male macro targets (equal 1/3 blend of the three ethnic targets, i.e. MakeHuman
gender=1.0, age=25, average muscle/weight); triangulated; one iteration of Loop subdivision;
exported with smooth vertex normals. No rescaling: units are MakeHuman decimetres.
